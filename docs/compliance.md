# Repair evidence checks

Checked 6 October 2026. These checks locate missing records and overdue internal tasks. They do not certify legal compliance, decide a consumer's remedy, or replace review of the circumstances.

| Rule | Record check | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| AU-NOTICE | Accepted AU repairs need written notice evidence dated no later than acceptance, a data-loss notice flag for devices storing user data, and a refurbished-parts flag where applicable. Assessment-only tickets are excluded. | [ACCC repair notices](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought/repair-notices): written notice is required before accepting relevant goods for repair. |
| NZ-SERVICE | Flag active NZ repairs past the agreed due date. | [Consumer Protection service guarantees](https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/guidance-for-businesses/complying-with-consumer-laws/obligations-under-the-consumer-guarantees-act): care, skill, purpose and reasonable time where no timeframe is agreed. A missed promised date is a review flag, not an automatic breach determination. |
| INT-APPROVAL | Missing approval reference after assessment. | Internal shop control, not a prescribed legal record format. |
| INT-QA | Ready or collected repair without check notes and checker. | Internal evidence of workmanship review. |
| INT-CLAIM | Open customer remedy follow-up past its chosen date. | Internal follow-up deadline, not a statutory time limit. |

Devices default to storing user data. A repair notice generated by `npm run docs` is a draft, never evidence that the consumer received it. Give a written notice with enough time for consideration before acceptance, then record the actual delivery time and a retained evidence reference. Never backdate missing evidence. The template includes the ACCC's prescribed refurbished-goods wording; review whether it matches the shop's practice. The CLI blocks new AU approvals without the recorded notice evidence.

Imported legacy work remains visible even when evidence is missing. Its gaps appear in the report. Approval records do not waive consumer rights. Shop warranty expiry does not end statutory guarantees; claims are accepted regardless of that date. See [NZ remedies](https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/general-help/how-to-complain/know-your-rights-to-a-refund-replacement-or-repair).

The free system has no device device passcodes, passwords or copied personal device content. Do not store those in notes. Operators manage access, retention, secure backups and physical devices separately. Payment processing, tax invoicing, e-waste rules, electrical certification and unattended device disposal are outside these checks.
